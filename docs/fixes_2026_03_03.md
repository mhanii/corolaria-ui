# Recent UI Fixes (2026-03-03)

This document summarizes the fixes implemented to resolve issues with chat status indicators and research visualization.

## 1. Document Generation Artifact Persistence

**Problem**: Artifact Chips generated during a chat stream would disappear once the stream completed, until the final message list fully re-rendered.
**Solution**: 
- Updated `src/components/chat/StreamingArea.tsx` to explicitly render `ArtifactChip` components as they are received in the stream.
- Modified `src/components/chat/StatusIndicator.tsx` to stop showing the generic "Loading Artifact" mockup once the `document_creation_completed` phase is reached. This ensures a smooth transition to the real, functional `ArtifactChip`.

---

## 2. ResearchView Dynamic Clusters

**Problem**: The research plan visualization only showed the initial 2 clusters (groups of steps) even if the research session involved more than 150 elements. It also failed to update properly when a "replan" occurred.
**Solution**:
- **Cluster Density**: Reduced `STEPS_PER_GROUP` from 5 to 2 in `src/components/chat/ResearchFlow.tsx`. This allows more clusters to be visible for the same number of steps, providing better visual density.
- **Replan Logic**: Updated the `cachedPlan` effect in `src/components/chat/StatusIndicator.tsx` to **append** new steps during a replan instead of overwriting the entire plan. This keeps the visual progress continuous.
- **Auto-Expansion**: If the backend moves beyond the known plan steps, `StatusIndicator` now dynamically injects a placeholder step ("Investigando en mayor profundidad...") to ensure the progress bar and circles keep advancing visually.

---

## 3. Fast-Forward Visual Completion

**Problem**: The research plan view remained visible for too long after the research phase ended, slowly "crawling" to the final step.
**Solution**: 
- Added a dedicated `useEffect` in `src/components/chat/StatusIndicator.tsx` that detects when the `isGenerationPhase` (the phase after research) begins.
- It immediately "snaps" the visual progress to the final step and sets a 500ms timer to dismiss the plan view, providing a much faster and more responsive transition to the assistant's final response.

---

## 4. Pre-existing Lint Error in Editor

**Problem**: A React Hook naming/conditional violation in `src/app/editor/page.tsx` was preventing production builds.
**Solution**: 
- Extracted an inlined `useCallback` from the JSX props into a properly declared constant at the top of the `EditorPage` component, ensuring it is always called in the same order.
